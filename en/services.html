FAQ

I only have a sketch or an idea. Can we still talk?

Yes. Sketches, photos or a written description are fine. We confirm the requirements with you first, then assess scope and send a quote.

Can we hire you for analysis only?

Yes. We can analyze your existing 3D models, or review the setup and results of analyses your own team has run.

Can the analysis report be used for acceptance or certification?

The report supports design decisions and improvements. It is not a third-party acceptance or certification document.

Will our data be kept confidential?

We can sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share any data.
