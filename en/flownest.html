FLOWNEST · TEAM FILE SYNC

Your team's design files, no longer overwriting each other.

FlowNest compares the work folder on your computer with your team's shared Google Drive folder, and lists what to upload, what to download, and what both sides changed. Every overwrite is backed up first, and a mistaken sync can be undone in one step.

One scan, four answers.

A project is a pair of folders: the work folder on your computer and the folder your whole team shares in the cloud. FlowNest compares the two by file size and modification time, and gives only four states.

  • ↑ Upload

    Your version is newer, or the team doesn't have this file yet.

  • ↓ Download

    A teammate uploaded a newer version, or you don't have this file yet.

  • ⚠ Both changed

    Never handled automatically. You decide which version to keep.

  • ✓ In sync

    Both sides hold the same version. Nothing to do.

FAQ

Do my files pass through your servers?

No. Files move only between your computer and your team's shared Google Drive folder.

Does it have to be Google Drive?

FlowNest is designed and tested for shared Google Drive folders. Every member's computer must see the shared folder in File Explorer, for example through Google Drive for desktop.

What happens to my files if I stop using it?

Nothing. They are ordinary files and stay in their original folders.

Is there a Mac version?

The trial is currently available for Windows. For the Mac version, please contact us by email.

Windows shows a security warning when I run it.

On first run, SmartScreen may appear. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

What if I change computers?

Enter the same serial on the new computer to activate it again. No new request is needed.
